About Obsidian

Where Code Meets
Competition

OBSIDIAN is the official hackathon platform of ACM DPGU Club — built for students who live to build, break, and innovate.

Like the rarest block in the Nether, great code is forged under pressure and fire. Compete in timed challenges, form unstoppable teams, and prove your skills on the leaderboard.

Every semester, we host themed hackathons ranging from 6-hour sprints to full 48-hour build sessions — open to all DPGU students.

How It Works

Your Path to Victory

01 🪪
Create Your Account

Sign up with your college email. Build your profile, add your stack, and join the DPGU hacker community.

02 ⚔️
Join a Hackathon

Browse upcoming events, register solo or form a team. Each hackathon has its own theme, rules, and prizes.

03 🏆
Build & Win

Submit your project, get judged by ACM mentors, and climb the leaderboard. Earn badges, XP, and glory.
